‎Abraham Lewysohn

‎and

‎to his elder brother  
‎Salomon Lewysohn  
‎in Posen,  
‎(author of the treatise: Chibbat Jeruschelajim we-Ahabat Zion, Wollstein 5615)

‎by the author.
