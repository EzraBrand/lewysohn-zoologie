# p. 68 (scan 82): places to check

Draft: Claude Opus 5.5 @low +ital, 31-Oct-2026. I read it against the 1000 px preview and found no
error in the German. Check these at full size (`68.scan.jpg`):

1. Line 1 and 7: `קפוף` (twice in the body, once more on line 10). Is it קפוף or קיפוף?
2. Line 26: the Arabic `لَبَأَ` with its vowel marks: are fatḥa on all three letters and the hamza
   printed as given?
3. Line 1–2: the doubled quotation marks `„„Gepriesen der, … verändert!““`. The print has a quote
   inside a quote; the second closing mark may be a different form.
4. Footnote `**)`: the order of the eight Hebrew words in `נשר, שחל, לביא, ארי, דוב, נמר, עש und כפיר`.
   In a right-to-left list with commas the logical order is easy to reverse. It must give the
   order a reader reads in the print (right to left), ending with כפיר before "verglichen".
5. Footnote `*)`: `§. 132, Anhang` — is "Anhang" in Fraktur, and is there a comma?
6. Hebrew-script words in the body: ארי, אריא, אריה, ארי זכר, לביה, גורייתא, אריות, אריותא, גוריא,
   ראה, ארה, לבי, גור, כלבא.

Transcription conventions (as in ocr.py): long s → s; letter-spaced words as plain text
(the print has "p h a n t" and "M e e r k a t z e" letter-spaced); line breaks and end-of-line
hyphens kept; Fraktur and Antiqua are not marked.
